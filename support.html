What Now Support

How to reach the developer, and answers to common questions about What Now for iPhone.

developer.emadrahman@gmail.com

Emails are answered by the developer, usually within a few business days. Contact Support in Settings › About fills in the app version, device model, iOS version, and your Pro status for you, which helps with any problem.

Common questions

The Live Activity doesn't appear

Check that What Now has Full Access in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Calendars, and that Live Activities are on in iOS Settings › Apps › What Now. If calendar access is blocked by Screen Time or a device profile, it has to be allowed there first. Without Pro, What Now follows one calendar, so make sure you've chosen the one your events are on.

The Live Activity appears an hour before your first event (with Pro, you can choose 15 minutes to 2 hours ahead, under Settings › Live Activity) and ends after a gap of more than four hours. All-day events don't count.

The Live Activity is out of date

Tap the critter on the Lock Screen to check your calendar again, or open the app. If you've hidden the critter under Appearance, open the app instead. To have it check at set times without opening the app, follow the Refresh Automatically guide in Settings › Live Activity, which sets up a Shortcuts automation. Keeping Background App Refresh on in iOS Settings › Apps › What Now also helps it stay current on its own. Events added on another device show up once your calendar has synced them to your iPhone.

An event is missing

What Now shows events you've accepted or made yourself. With Pro, you can also include "Maybe" events and unanswered invitations under Settings › Filters. Also check that the event's calendar is chosen (without Pro, What Now follows one calendar), that none of your Hidden Keywords is in its title, and that its eye button in the agenda isn't crossed out. Events on a calendar set to App Only stay in the agenda and show on the Live Activity only if you tap their eye button. All-day events show in the agenda but never on the Live Activity.

There's no Join button

One-tap Join is part of What Now Pro, on the Live Activity, in the Dynamic Island, and in the agenda. The call link has to be in the event's URL, location, or notes. Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, Webex, FaceTime, Slack huddles, Whereby, Jitsi, Amazon Chime, and GoTo Meeting links are recognized.

My Pro purchase isn't recognized

Open Settings › What Now Pro and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure your iPhone is signed in to the Apple Account you bought Pro with: purchases belong to the Apple Account, not the device. If the purchase is waiting for approval, such as Ask to Buy, Pro unlocks on its own once it's approved.

Deleting your data

What Now keeps only its settings on your iPhone, and deleting the app removes them. Your calendar itself is never changed. If you'd rather not send anonymous usage counts, turn off Share Anonymous Usage in the app under Settings › About. The privacy policy has the details.

Reporting a problem

Say what you did, what happened, and what you expected, and use Contact Support in the app so the version and device details come along. A screenshot of the Lock Screen helps with anything about how the Live Activity looks.
